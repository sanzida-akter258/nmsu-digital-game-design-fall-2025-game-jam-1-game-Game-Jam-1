# Monster Mash: Game Design Document
## Vision Statement
A competitive card game where two players fight each other with monsters of their own creation.

## Gameplay
### Mechanics
Two players draw 5 cards each. They can create a monster using leg cards, arm cards, and head cards. They will then attack the other player in the attack phase. 
### Scoring and/or Win / Lose Conditions
Lose Condition: The player who loses all of their health first will lose. 
Win Condition: Last player standing wins
### Controls
Players will have a build phase where a player will play the cards from their hands. 
### Aesthetic
Mad scientist, monster cards will have a very “mixed media” styling to them as various members in the group will be designing the visual element of cards. 
### Desired Player Experience
What should be the emotional experience of playing this game? // Madness? 
A strategic experience of waiting on the right cards to build a monster to try and counter the opponent, waiting for the right moment to build their monster
## Game Characters
Who are the characters of your game? Include both player characters (PCs) and non-player characters (NPCs).
* Players
* Monsters 
* Five monster types: Zombie, werewolf, robot, dragon, and plant creature
## Story
If your game includes a story, describe it here. Provide a short narrative describing what is happening in your game?
Two mad scientists have decided to compete and see who can make the strongest monster.
## The Game World
The game takes place in a chaotic mad scientist's laboratory, where two scientists compete to create the strongest monster. The board represents their experimental battleground, where players combine different monster body parts to build their creatures and fight against each other. The overall theme is madness, with strange experiments, mismatched monster parts, and a colorful, chaotic atmosphere.
## Media List
Provide some direction for the art, what should it look like?
The artwork will follow a mad scientist theme with a chaotic, colorful, and mixed-media style. The monster cards will have unique designs, combining different body parts to create strange and unusual creatures. The overall design should reflect the madness theme while keeping the cards and board easy to understand.
